About

Technical icons for marine and building services.

Synthera designs digital assets for engineers, naval architects, facility teams and operators who need clear symbols in SCADA and BMS screens, tenders and training decks.

This specimen shows the line style used across the full set: round caps and joins, a shared live area, and silhouettes tuned to stay readable at 24 pixels.
